# Rule: Zettelkasten Navigation Protocol

**Core Philosophy:** The file explorer is a storage warehouse, not a map. Do not use folders to find information. 

To navigate this vault, strictly use the following three methods:

### 1. Browse via MOCs (Map of Content)
*   **When to use:** When you need to study a broad topic or see how concepts relate to a specific subject (e.g., DSI or the Google Cybersecurity course).
*   **How:** Open the specific MOC note. Rely on the Dataview tables to list the relevant Fleeting, Reference, and Permanent notes automatically. 
*   **Mindset:** MOCs are your dynamic, virtual folders. 

### 2. Jump via Quick Switcher (`Ctrl + O`)
*   **When to use:** When you know the exact name of the concept you are looking for (e.g., "Threat Actors", "VLAN", or "SOLID").
*   **How:** Press `Ctrl + O` and type the title or the alias. 
*   **Mindset:** This is your teleportation tool. The physical folder location of the file is irrelevant; the title and aliases are all that matter.

### 3. Deep Search via the Magnifying Glass (`Ctrl + Shift + F`)
*   **When to use:** When you are looking for a specific keyword, tag (like `#architecture` or `#security`), or phrase buried inside the text of your notes.
*   **How:** Click the magnifying glass in the top left corner (or use the shortcut) to search the entire database. 
*   **Mindset:** This is your SQL query for raw text when the title or MOC isn't enough.
